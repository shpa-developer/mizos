Frequently asked questions

Is Mizos free?
Yes, Mizos is completely free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden charges. All features are available at no cost.
Do I need an account to use Mizos?
No. Mizos works without any sign-up or account creation. All your measurement data is stored locally on your device. Just install and start saving measurements.
How do I share measurements with my tailor?
Tap the share button on any measurement, and send it via WhatsApp or any messaging app. Your tailor receives a clean, formatted message with all the numbers plus a link to view them on the web. No app needed on their end.
What measurements can I save in the app?
Mizos has pre-built templates for T-shirts, shirts, suits/blazers, pants, shoes, eyeglasses, rings, bangles, and general body measurements. Each template includes the right fields for that category.
Does Mizos work without internet?
Yes. All measurement data is stored locally on your phone using on-device storage. You can add, edit, and view measurements without any internet connection. You only need internet when you choose to share measurements via messaging apps.
