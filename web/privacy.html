Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Summary

Mizos stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We have no servers.

Data we collect

None. Mizos does not collect any personal data. All measurements, profiles, and preferences are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (SQLite).

Data sharing

When you use the "Share" feature, you choose to send measurement data to another person via your preferred messaging app (WhatsApp, SMS, etc.). This sharing is initiated by you and goes directly to the recipient — it does not pass through our servers.

Shared links open a static web page hosted on mizos.in that displays the measurement data encoded in the URL itself. No data is stored on our web server.

Contacts permission

Mizos requests access to your phone contacts solely to let you link a profile to a contact for convenience (auto-fill name, show phone number). Contact data is read locally and never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Mizos does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

Children's privacy

Mizos does not knowingly collect any data from children. Since we collect no data from anyone, this applies universally.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Deleting individual profiles or measurements within the app
  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@mizos.in.
